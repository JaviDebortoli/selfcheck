# Demo video script (30 seconds, English subtitles)

Recorded on the running system (`docker compose up --build`), screen capture at 1280×800 (quick view) and
1920×1080 (control center). Reset the mission before recording (simulator screen → *Reset mission*).

| Time | Screen and action | English subtitle |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0–4 s | Quick view, Crew member A, everything green, ECG beating. | SelfCheck: crew health at a glance, in real time. |
| 4–8 s | Simulator screen: trigger **Arrhythmia** for Crew member B, speed ×60. | The ship's monitors stream every vital sign on mission time. |
| 8–13 s | Quick view of Crew member B: premature beats (yellow), then ventricular tachycardia — the ECG changes shape and the panel turns red. | Sourced medical rules turn each value into a state. |
| 13–19 s | Simulator: trigger **Solar event**, speed ×3600. Control center: the whole crew goes yellow, then red at 250 mSv; the alert log shows the high-priority combination alert. | A solar particle event is checked against NASA's 250 mSv limit. |
| 19–25 s | Tap the radiological card: bottom sheet with what it measures and what to do. Switch the language to Spanish. | Every indicator explains what it measures and what to do — in English and Spanish. |
| 25–30 s | Control center overview, habitat panel with the dust "?" icon. Fade to the SelfCheck logo. | Three environments, never green by default. Open source. |

Notes
